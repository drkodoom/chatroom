START A CHAT GAME

Boss health scales with the number of people online. Every player attack is followed by a boss counterattack.

Werewolf: all people online when the game begins become players. One werewolf is chosen at random with 4–6 players; two are chosen with 7 or more. Roles are sent privately by the server.

At night, werewolves secretly type /kill Name. During the day, living players discuss in chat and type /vote Name. Villagers win by eliminating every werewolf. Werewolves win when they equal or outnumber the remaining villagers.

RPS Tournament: opens room registration. Players type /rps join. Admin/MOD starts the bracket when ready. Picks stay secret until both players lock in.

Tournament matches are best-of-3; the championship is best-of-5. The winner earns the temporary reigning RPS Champion title for up to 30 days.
